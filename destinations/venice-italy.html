About Venice

Venice, the floating city, is one of the world's most unique and romantic destinations. Built on more than 100 small islands in a lagoon in the Adriatic Sea, Venice is a masterpiece of architectural and engineering ingenuity.

The city is crisscrossed by more than 150 canals connected by over 400 bridges, creating a magical atmosphere where cars are nonexistent and boats replace buses. Venice's rich history as a powerful maritime republic is evident in its magnificent palaces, churches, and public buildings.

Beyond its famous canals and gondolas, Venice offers hidden treasures around every corner, from quiet squares (campielli) to artisan workshops showcasing traditional crafts like glassblowing and lace-making.

Whether you're gliding down the Grand Canal in a vaporetto, getting lost in the labyrinthine streets, or admiring masterpieces by Titian and Tintoretto, Venice offers an unforgettable experience unlike any other city in the world.

Top Attractions in Venice

Grand Canal

Venice's main waterway lined with magnificent palaces, churches, and historic buildings, best viewed by vaporetto or water taxi.

St. Mark's Square

The city's principal square surrounded by some of Venice's most important buildings, including St. Mark's Basilica and the Doge's Palace.

Doge's Palace

Gothic masterpiece that was the residence of the Doge of Venice, now a museum showcasing the city's history and art.

Rialto Bridge

Oldest of the four bridges spanning the Grand Canal, an architectural marvel and bustling commercial area.

Murano Island

Island famous for its glassmaking tradition, with factories where visitors can watch artisans create beautiful glass objects.

Burano Island

Colorful fishing village known for its brightly painted houses and traditional lace-making.

Must Try Cuisine in Venice

Cicchetti

Venetian version of tapas - small snacks or side dishes served in traditional bars called bàcari.

Sarde in Saor

Marinated sardines with onions, raisins, pine nuts, and vinegar, a classic Venetian dish.

Bigoli in Salsa

Thick whole-wheat pasta served with a rich onion and anchovy sauce, a traditional Venetian first course.

Tiramisu

Famous Italian dessert said to have originated in Treviso near Venice, with layers of coffee-soaked ladyfingers and mascarpone.

Best Time to Visit

Spring

March - May (12-18°C)

Pleasant weather with fewer crowds. Ideal for sightseeing and outdoor activities.

Summer

June - August (22-30°C)

Warm and humid with occasional thunderstorms. Peak tourist season with high prices and crowds.

Fall

September - November (12-20°C)

Mild temperatures and beautiful light. Great time for photography with fewer tourists.

Winter

December - February (0-8°C)

Cool with possible flooding (acqua alta). Fewer tourists but some attractions may have reduced hours.

Travel Tips & Cost Estimate

Travel Tips

  • Getting Around

    Walk whenever possible - it's the best way to discover hidden corners. Use vaporettos (water buses) for longer distances.

  • Getting Lost

    Embrace getting lost in Venice's labyrinthine streets - it's part of the experience. Carry a paper map as backup.

  • Skip Lines

    Book tickets online for major attractions like St. Mark's Basilica and the Doge's Palace to avoid long queues.

  • Acqua Alta

    Check the acqua alta (high water) forecast. Pack waterproof boots or buy disposable boot covers if flooding is expected.
