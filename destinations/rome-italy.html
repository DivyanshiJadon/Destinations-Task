About Rome

Rome, the Eternal City, is a living museum where ancient ruins stand alongside Renaissance masterpieces and modern life. As the capital of Italy and once the heart of the Roman Empire, Rome's influence on Western civilization cannot be overstated.

Walking through Rome is like traveling through time. The Colosseum, Roman Forum, and Pantheon tell stories of gladiators and emperors, while the Vatican City houses some of the world's greatest artistic treasures.

Beyond its historical significance, Rome is a city of vibrant piazzas, charming trattorias, and passionate locals. The Roman culinary tradition elevates simple ingredients to extraordinary dishes that have been perfected over centuries.

Whether you're tossing coins into the Trevi Fountain, admiring Michelangelo's Sistine Chapel, or enjoying gelato on the Spanish Steps, Rome offers an intoxicating blend of history, art, and Italian la dolce vita.

Top Attractions in Rome

Colosseum

Iconic ancient amphitheater where gladiators once fought, symbol of Roman engineering prowess.

Vatican Museums

World-renowned museum complex featuring the Sistine Chapel and Renaissance masterpieces.

Trevi Fountain

Baroque masterpiece where tradition says tossing a coin ensures your return to Rome.

Roman Forum

Ancient city center with ruins of government buildings and temples from the Roman Republic.

Pantheon

Best-preserved ancient Roman building with its massive dome and oculus.

Spanish Steps

Elegant 18th-century stairway connecting Piazza di Spagna with Trinità dei Monti church.

Must Try Cuisine in Rome

Cacio e Pepe

Classic Roman pasta with pecorino cheese and black pepper - simplicity perfected.

Carbonara

Rich pasta with eggs, guanciale, pecorino, and black pepper - no cream!

Supplì

Fried rice balls with tomato sauce and mozzarella, Rome's beloved street food.

Gelato

Authentic Italian ice cream made fresh daily with natural ingredients.

Best Time to Visit

Spring

March - May (12-20°C)

Perfect weather with fewer crowds, ideal for outdoor sightseeing and exploring ruins.

Summer

June - August (22-32°C)

Hot and crowded but vibrant with outdoor events and long evening strolls.

Fall

September - November (15-24°C)

Pleasant temperatures and golden light perfect for photography and exploration.

Winter

December - February (8-14°C)

Mild winters with fewer tourists, great for museums and cozy trattorias.

Travel Tips & Cost Estimate

Travel Tips

  • Skip-the-Line

    Book tickets online in advance for major attractions to avoid hours-long queues.

  • Dress Code

    Cover shoulders and knees when visiting churches and the Vatican. Bring a light scarf.

  • Free Water

    Drink from Rome's nasoni fountains - the water is clean, cold, and free throughout the city.

  • Dining Hours

    Lunch is 12:30-2:30pm, dinner from 8pm. Many restaurants close between services.
