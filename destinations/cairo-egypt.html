About Cairo

Cairo, the sprawling capital of Egypt, is a city where ancient wonders meet modern urban life. Known as the 'City of a Thousand Minarets,' Cairo offers a fascinating journey through thousands of years of history, from the time of the Pharaohs to the present day.

The city is home to some of the world's most iconic ancient monuments, including the Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx. Beyond these ancient wonders, Cairo boasts a rich Islamic heritage with historic mosques, madrassas, and the world's oldest bazaars.

Cairo's chaotic energy is part of its charm, with bustling streets filled with honking cars, vendors, and people going about their daily lives. The city's culinary scene is a delight for the senses, with aromatic spices, grilled meats, and sweet pastries.

Whether you're marveling at ancient pyramids, exploring historic mosques, bargaining in bustling souks, or cruising the Nile, Cairo offers an unforgettable journey through one of the world's great civilizations.

Top Attractions in Cairo

Pyramids of Giza

Ancient wonders of the world, including the Great Pyramid, the last surviving wonder of the ancient world.

Egyptian Museum

Home to the world's largest collection of Pharaonic antiquities, including the treasures of Tutankhamun.

Khan el-Khalili

Historic bazaar dating back to the 14th century, selling everything from spices to souvenirs in a maze of narrow alleys.

Citadel of Saladin

Medieval Islamic fortress offering panoramic views of Cairo and housing several museums and historic mosques.

Al-Azhar Mosque

One of Cairo's oldest mosques and a masterpiece of Islamic architecture, dating back to 970 AD.

Nile River Cruise

Scenic boat ride offering a different perspective of the city and a relaxing escape from the bustling streets.

Must Try Cuisine in Cairo

Koshari

Egypt's national dish made with rice, lentils, chickpeas, and pasta topped with spicy tomato sauce and crispy onions.

Ful Medames

Traditional breakfast dish of fava beans cooked with spices and olive oil, typically eaten with bread.

Shawarma

Popular street food of marinated meat cooked on a vertical spit and served in pita bread with vegetables and sauces.

Umm Ali

Delicious Egyptian dessert similar to bread pudding, made with pastry, milk, nuts, and raisins.

Best Time to Visit

Spring

March - May (15-28°C)

Pleasant weather with mild temperatures. Ideal for sightseeing and outdoor activities.

Summer

June - August (25-38°C)

Very hot and dry. Early morning and evening sightseeing recommended. Fewer tourists.

Fall

September - November (18-30°C)

Comfortable temperatures with less humidity. Great time to visit with pleasant weather.

Winter

December - February (8-20°C)

Mild days and cool nights. Can be rainy but generally pleasant for sightseeing.

Travel Tips & Cost Estimate

Travel Tips

  • Dress Modestly

    Cover shoulders and knees, especially when visiting religious sites. Women should carry a scarf for mosques.

  • Currency

    Have small bills for tipping (baksheesh) which is expected for most services. Carry Egyptian pounds for small purchases.

  • Bargaining

    Bargaining is expected in markets. Start at about half the asking price and negotiate politely.

  • Stay Hydrated

    Cairo's climate is dry. Drink plenty of bottled water and protect yourself from the sun.
