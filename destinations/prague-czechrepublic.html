Prague,

Czech Republic

About Prague

Prague, the City of a Hundred Spires, is a fairytale city that seems frozen in time. The capital of the Czech Republic boasts one of Europe's best-preserved historic centers, with stunning architecture spanning a thousand years.

The city's skyline is dominated by the majestic Prague Castle complex, while the Vltava River is crossed by the beautiful Charles Bridge. Prague's cobblestone streets, Gothic churches, and colorful baroque buildings create an enchanting atmosphere that captivates visitors.

Beyond its architectural beauty, Prague has a rich cultural heritage, being home to famous figures like Franz Kafka and Mozart, who premiered his opera Don Giovanni here. The city's beer culture is legendary, with historic breweries serving some of the best beer in the world.

Whether you're exploring the castle complex, crossing the Charles Bridge at sunrise, wandering through the Jewish Quarter, or simply enjoying a beer in a historic pub, Prague offers a magical experience that feels like stepping back in time.

Top Attractions in Prague

Prague Castle

Largest ancient castle complex in the world, housing St. Vitus Cathedral, Old Royal Palace, and Golden Lane.

Charles Bridge

Historic stone bridge across the Vltava River, adorned with 30 statues and offering stunning views of the city.

Old Town Square

Historic square featuring the Astronomical Clock, Church of Our Lady before Týn, and colorful baroque buildings.

Jewish Quarter

Historic Jewish ghetto with several synagogues, the Old Jewish Cemetery, and the Jewish Museum.

Vyšehrad

Historic fort overlooking the Vltava River, home to the Slavín cemetery and the Basilica of St. Peter and St. Paul.

Dancing House

Modern architectural masterpiece standing out among the city's historic buildings, inspired by Fred Astaire and Ginger Rogers.

Must Try Cuisine in Prague

Svíčková

Traditional Czech dish of marinated sirloin with cream sauce, served with bread dumplings and cranberry compote.

Trdelník

Sweet pastry made from rolled dough wrapped around a stick, grilled, and coated with sugar and walnuts.

Pilsner Beer

World-famous Czech beer originating in Plzeň, known for its golden color and balanced flavor.

Vepřo knedlo zelo

Roast pork with dumplings and sauerkraut, a hearty traditional Czech dish.

Best Time to Visit

Spring

March - May (8-18°C)

Mild weather with blooming parks and gardens. Ideal for walking tours and outdoor sightseeing.

Summer

June - August (18-28°C)

Warm and sunny with long days. Peak tourist season with lively outdoor beer gardens and festivals.

Fall

September - November (8-18°C)

Pleasant temperatures and beautiful fall colors. Great time for photography with fewer tourists.

Winter

December - February (-2-4°C)

Cold with occasional snow. Christmas markets create a magical atmosphere. Fewer tourists and lower prices.

Travel Tips & Cost Estimate

Travel Tips

  • Walking

    Prague's historic center is best explored on foot. Wear comfortable shoes for cobblestone streets.

  • Currency

    Use Czech koruna (CZK) rather than euros. Credit cards are widely accepted but carry cash for smaller establishments.

  • Beer Culture

    Beer is cheaper than water in many pubs. Try different local brews and enjoy the beer garden culture.

  • Skip Lines

    Buy tickets online for major attractions like Prague Castle and the Jewish Museum to avoid long queues.
