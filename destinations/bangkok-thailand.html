About Bangkok

Bangkok, Thailand's vibrant capital, is a city of dazzling temples, bustling markets, and vibrant street life. Known as the 'City of Angels,' Bangkok offers a sensory feast that combines ancient traditions with modern urban energy.

The city's skyline is dominated by golden temples and modern skyscrapers, while its streets buzz with tuk-tuks, street food vendors, and colorful markets. Bangkok's rich cultural heritage is evident in its ornate temples, traditional performances, and royal palaces.

Beyond its cultural attractions, Bangkok is a food lover's paradise, with street food stalls on every corner serving authentic Thai cuisine. The city's nightlife is equally diverse, from sophisticated rooftop bars to bustling night markets.

Whether you're exploring the Grand Palace, cruising the Chao Phraya River, shopping at floating markets, or indulging in a traditional Thai massage, Bangkok offers an unforgettable Southeast Asian experience.

Top Attractions in Bangkok

Grand Palace

Stunning complex of buildings that served as the official residence of Thai kings for 150 years.

Wat Arun

Beautiful temple on the west bank of the Chao Phraya River, known for its impressive spires covered in colorful porcelain.

Wat Pho

Temple famous for its giant reclining Buddha measuring 46 meters long and covered in gold leaf.

Chatuchak Weekend Market

One of the world's largest weekend markets with over 15,000 stalls selling everything from clothing to antiques.

Floating Markets

Traditional markets where vendors sell food and goods from boats, offering a glimpse into old Thai ways of life.

Khao San Road

Famous backpacker street lined with budget guesthouses, street food stalls, bars, and travel agencies.

Must Try Cuisine in Bangkok

Pad Thai

Stir-fried rice noodle dish commonly served as a street food, with eggs, tofu, tamarind pulp, and fish sauce.

Tom Yum Goong

Hot and sour soup with shrimp, lemongrass, kaffir lime leaves, galangal, and chili peppers.

Green Curry

Creamy curry made with green chili paste, coconut milk, meat, eggplant, and Thai basil.

Mango Sticky Rice

Popular dessert made with glutinous rice, fresh mango slices, and sweet coconut milk sauce.

Best Time to Visit

Spring

February - April (27-35°C)

Hot and dry with occasional rain. Perfect for temple visits but can be uncomfortably hot.

Summer

May - July (28-35°C)

Hot and humid with heavy rainfall. Fewer tourists but some outdoor activities may be limited.

Fall

August - October (27-33°C)

Rainy season with possible flooding, but also lush green landscapes and fewer crowds.

Winter

November - January (25-32°C)

Best time to visit with cooler temperatures, less humidity, and minimal rainfall.

Travel Tips & Cost Estimate

Travel Tips

  • Dress Code

    Cover shoulders and knees when visiting temples. Remove shoes before entering temple buildings.

  • Public Transport

    Use the BTS Skytrain and MRT subway to avoid traffic. Tuk-tuks are fun but negotiate the price before riding.

  • Street Food

    Don't miss Bangkok's street food. Look for busy stalls with high turnover for the freshest options.

  • Bargaining

    Bargaining is expected in markets. Start at about half the asking price and negotiate politely.
