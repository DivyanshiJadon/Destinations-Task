About Istanbul

Istanbul, where Europe meets Asia, is a city of fascinating contrasts and rich history. As the former capital of three empires, Istanbul straddles two continents with the Bosphorus Strait flowing through its heart.

The city's skyline is dominated by magnificent minarets and Byzantine churches, while its bustling bazaars and vibrant neighborhoods offer a sensory feast. Istanbul's unique position has made it a melting pot of cultures, religions, and cuisines.

From the awe-inspiring Hagia Sophia to the bustling Grand Bazaar, Istanbul offers a journey through time. The city's culinary scene is equally impressive, with influences from across the Mediterranean and Middle East.

Whether you're exploring ancient palaces, cruising the Bosphorus, or sipping Turkish coffee in a historic café, Istanbul captivates with its blend of Eastern and Western traditions.

Top Attractions in Istanbul

Hagia Sophia

Architectural marvel that has served as a cathedral, mosque, and now museum, showcasing Istanbul's diverse history.

Blue Mosque

Stunning mosque with six minarets and exquisite blue Iznik tiles adorning its interior.

Topkapi Palace

Opulent former residence of Ottoman sultans, housing treasures and offering panoramic views.

Grand Bazaar

One of the world's oldest and largest covered markets, with thousands of shops selling everything from spices to jewelry.

Bosphorus Cruise

Scenic boat ride along the strait separating Europe and Asia, passing palaces and waterfront mansions.

Basilica Cistern

Ancient underground water reservoir with atmospheric lighting and Medusa head columns.

Must Try Cuisine in Istanbul

Kebabs

Grilled meat skewers, often served with rice, vegetables, and yogurt sauce.

Baklava

Sweet pastry made of layers of filo filled with chopped nuts and sweetened with syrup or honey.

Turkish Delight

Gel-like confection made from starch and sugar, often flavored with rosewater, citrus, or nuts.

Meze

Selection of small dishes served as appetizers, including hummus, dolmas, and stuffed grape leaves.

Best Time to Visit

Spring

March - May (10-20°C)

Mild weather with blooming flowers, ideal for sightseeing and outdoor activities.

Summer

June - August (20-30°C)

Warm and sunny, perfect for Bosphorus cruises but can be crowded with tourists.

Fall

September - November (15-25°C)

Pleasant temperatures and fewer crowds, excellent for exploring historical sites.

Winter

December - February (5-10°C)

Cool and rainy, but cozy indoor attractions and fewer tourists at major sites.

Travel Tips & Cost Estimate

Travel Tips

  • Dress Respectfully

    Cover shoulders and knees when visiting mosques. Women should bring a scarf to cover their hair.

  • Currency

    Use Turkish Lira for small purchases. Credit cards are widely accepted in tourist areas.

  • Bargaining

    Bargaining is expected in the Grand Bazaar and other markets. Start at about half the asking price.

  • Istanbulkart

    Get an Istanbulkart card for discounted travel on public transportation including trams, metros, and ferries.
