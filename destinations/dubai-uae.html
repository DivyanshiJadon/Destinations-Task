About Dubai

Dubai, the jewel of the United Arab Emirates, is a city that defies imagination with its ultramodern architecture, luxury shopping, and ambitious vision. Rising from the desert sands, Dubai has transformed into a global hub for business, tourism, and innovation.

The city is home to the world's tallest building, largest shopping mall, and most luxurious hotels. Yet Dubai also preserves its rich Bedouin heritage through traditional souks, cultural districts, and desert experiences that showcase Arabian hospitality.

From skiing indoors to desert safaris, from Michelin-starred dining to street food, Dubai offers experiences that push the boundaries of possibility. The city's skyline, dominated by the iconic Burj Khalifa, represents humanity's architectural ambition.

Whether you're seeking luxury, adventure, culture, or business opportunities, Dubai delivers on every front with its characteristic blend of traditional Arabian charm and futuristic vision.

Top Attractions in Dubai

Burj Khalifa

The world's tallest building with observation decks offering stunning 360-degree city views.

Dubai Mall

Massive shopping center with aquarium, ice rink, and hundreds of luxury brands.

Palm Jumeirah

Iconic man-made palm-shaped island with luxury resorts and residences.

Dubai Marina

Stunning waterfront district with skyscrapers, restaurants, and yacht-filled waters.

Gold Souk

Traditional market with hundreds of shops selling gold, diamonds, and precious stones.

Desert Safari

Thrilling dune bashing, camel rides, and traditional Bedouin camp experiences.

Must Try Cuisine in Dubai

Shawarma

Marinated meat wrapped in flatbread with vegetables and tahini sauce.

Hummus

Creamy chickpea dip with olive oil, perfect with warm pita bread.

Machboos

Traditional Emirati rice dish with spiced meat or fish and dried limes.

Kunafa

Sweet cheese pastry soaked in syrup, a beloved Middle Eastern dessert.

Best Time to Visit

Spring

March - May (25-35°C)

Warm and pleasant, perfect for outdoor activities before the summer heat arrives.

Summer

June - August (35-45°C)

Extremely hot weather, but great indoor attractions and shopping festivals with huge discounts.

Fall

September - November (25-35°C)

Pleasant temperatures return, ideal for beach activities and desert excursions.

Winter

December - February (18-25°C)

Perfect weather with sunny days, ideal for all outdoor activities and shopping festivals.

Travel Tips & Cost Estimate

Travel Tips

  • Dress Modestly

    Respect local customs by covering shoulders and knees, especially in religious areas and malls.

  • Alcohol Laws

    Alcohol is served only in licensed venues. Public intoxication is illegal and heavily penalized.

  • Ramadan

    During Ramadan, eating, drinking, and smoking in public is prohibited during daylight hours.

  • Public Transport

    Use the Dubai Metro for efficient travel. Taxis are affordable but traffic can be heavy.
