About Seoul

Seoul, the dynamic capital of South Korea, is a city where ancient traditions meet cutting-edge technology. From historic palaces to futuristic districts, Seoul offers a fascinating blend of old and new that creates a uniquely Korean experience.

The city's rich history is preserved in its five grand palaces, traditional villages, and Buddhist temples, while its modern side is showcased in gleaming skyscrapers, high-tech shopping districts, and K-pop culture. Seoul's efficient public transportation makes it easy to explore its diverse neighborhoods.

Korean culture is deeply embedded in everyday life in Seoul, from the ubiquitous kimchi to the importance of respect in social interactions. The city's culinary scene is exceptional, with everything from street food to Michelin-starred restaurants.

Whether you're exploring ancient palaces, hiking in urban mountains, shopping in trendy districts, or indulging in Korean barbecue and skincare, Seoul offers a perfect blend of tradition and modernity.

Top Attractions in Seoul

Gyeongbokgung Palace

Largest of Seoul's five grand palaces, home to the Joseon Dynasty and featuring traditional architecture and changing of the guard ceremony.

N Seoul Tower

Iconic communication tower offering panoramic views of the city and famous for its 'love locks' tradition.

Bukchon Hanok Village

Traditional Korean village with hundreds of hanoks (traditional houses), offering a glimpse into old Seoul.

Myeongdong

Bustling shopping district with countless cosmetic shops, fashion boutiques, and street food stalls.

Insadong

Cultural district known for traditional crafts, art galleries, tea houses, and souvenir shops.

Hongdae

Youthful neighborhood near Hongik University known for its indie music culture, street performances, and trendy cafes.

Must Try Cuisine in Seoul

Korean BBQ

Grilled meat dishes, typically beef or pork, cooked at the table and served with various side dishes (banchan).

Kimchi

Fermented vegetable dish, usually made with cabbage and radish, served with nearly every Korean meal.

Bibimbap

Rice bowl topped with vegetables, meat, egg, and gochujang (chili pepper paste), mixed before eating.

Tteokbokki

Spicy stir-fried rice cakes in a sweet and spicy sauce, a popular Korean street food.

Best Time to Visit

Spring

March - May (10-20°C)

Mild weather with cherry blossoms in April. Perfect for outdoor activities and palace visits.

Summer

June - August (22-30°C)

Hot and humid with occasional rain. Great for indoor attractions and air-conditioned malls.

Fall

September - November (10-20°C)

Best time to visit with comfortable temperatures, clear skies, and beautiful fall foliage.

Winter

December - February (-5-5°C)

Cold with occasional snow. Great for skiing, hot springs, and indoor markets.

Travel Tips & Cost Estimate

Travel Tips

  • T-money Card

    Get a T-money card for convenient payment on public transport and in convenience stores.

  • Free WiFi

    Seoul has extensive free WiFi coverage in public areas, subway stations, and cafes.

  • Dining Etiquette

    Wait for elders to start eating. Don't leave chopsticks standing in rice. Use both hands when pouring drinks.

  • Tax Refund

    Get a tax refund on purchases over ₩30,000 at designated tax refund counters at the airport.
